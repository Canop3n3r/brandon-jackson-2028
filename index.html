About

A Californian for limited government

Brandon Jackson is a California Libertarian seeking the Libertarian Party’s presidential nomination. His campaign is built on a simple idea: government should protect your rights, not manage your life.

He serves on the Executive Committee of the Libertarian Party of California. A self-described tech wizard, he is the owner and CEO of Blacksuit Industries, which he has led since August 2021. He is a graduate of Arizona State University, class of 2018.

A defender of civil liberties, he was targeted for protest — and knows firsthand why limits on government power matter.

Issues

A platform for a freer country

Standard Libertarian planks to start from — a government bound by the Constitution, a peaceful foreign policy, and the right of every person to live, work, and speak without asking permission.

01

Civil liberties & the Fourth Amendment

End warrantless surveillance. Defend due process, free speech, and the right to be left alone. A free people should not have to live as if they are always being watched.

02

End the wars

A non-interventionist foreign policy. Bring the troops home, stop endless wars, and pursue peace through trade and diplomacy — not occupation. Ending the wars doesn’t mean weakening our defense: America should still have the most advanced defensive military in the world.

03

Cut spending & taxes

The federal government spends too much and takes too much. Keep Americans’ tax dollars at home instead of shipping them overseas, and balance the budget by shrinking the state — not by squeezing the people who earn the money.

04

End the drug war & reform criminal justice

End federal prohibition. Stop ruining lives over consensual choices. Focus courts and prisons on force and fraud, and restore a justice system worthy of a free country.

05

The Second Amendment

The right to keep and bear arms is a right, not a privilege. No federal gun registry, and no ban on ownership by law-abiding people.

06

Sound money, crypto & AI

Sound money and an end to inflationary abuse. Protect the right to hold, build, and use cryptocurrency without permission or a central bank digital currency. Keep AI open and in the hands of the people, not licensed to a few — and never turned into a tool for mass surveillance.
